Earth terrain — NASA Blue Marble Next Generation, August 2004

Source: NASA Earth Observatory, with shaded topography and bathymetry.
https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography-bathymetry/
Retrieved: 2026-11-11. A satellite composite, not live observations.

Native 500 m source: eight 21600 x 21600 geographic RGB images, A1 through D2.
The full geographic raster is 86400 x 43200 pixels. North is at the top;
longitude spans -180 to +180, latitude +90 to -90.
Exact source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in terrain-tiles/source.json.

tools/build_terrain.py creates a local four-level pyramid:
level 0: 10800 x 5400 (4 km); level 1: 21600 x 10800 (2 km);
level 2: 43200 x 21600 (1 km); level 3: 86400 x 43200 (native 500 m).
Each tile has 1350 x 1350 content pixels plus a one-pixel gutter containing
real neighboring image data. Longitude wraps; latitude clamps at the poles.
Lower levels use LANCZOS downsampling. RGB JPEG quality 90, no chroma subsampling.
No artificial sharpening, generated terrain, or upscaled detail is used.

earth-day.jpg (8192 x 4096) and earth-day-mobile.jpg (4096 x 2048) are
overview textures derived from NASA's 21600 x 10800 global image:
https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography-bathymetry/august/world.topo.bathy.200408.3x21600x10800.jpg

The renderer loads only the visible region, with four concurrent requests
and at most twenty-five color tiles in GPU memory (sixteen on mobile,
fewer on older devices).
Detail fades in over the overview. Existing color treatment, atmosphere,
night shading and warm city lights are applied separately and retained.
