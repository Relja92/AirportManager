night-tiles/{level}/{column}-{row}.jpg — a geographic detail pyramid:
level 0: 21600 x 10800 global (2 km), level 1: 43200 x 21600 (1 km),
level 2: 86400 x 43200 (native 500 m).
Each tile contains 1350 x 1350 source pixels and one actual neighboring pixel
on every edge. Longitude wraps and latitude clamps at the world boundaries.
Empty tiles are recorded in the manifest and require no download.
Generated with tools/build_night_lights.py using Pillow, LANCZOS for the two
lower resolutions, and grayscale JPEG quality 95. No upscaling, artificial
sharpening or synthesized lights are applied.

The renderer selects a complete visible region at the finest level that fits
a bounded single-channel texture atlas (at most 36 tiles, fewer on older GPUs).
Only four images download at a time; the small overview remains available
while detail arrives. Desktop and mobile use the same close-zoom imagery.
The renderer applies a warm tint, suppresses near-black compression noise, and
smoothly fades the observed light intensity with the game's simulated sunlight.
No settlements or connecting roads have been synthesized.
